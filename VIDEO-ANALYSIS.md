# Reference videolar tahlili

## 1. Umumiy pattern

- Barcha videolar 9:16 vertical formatda, telefon uchun yaratilgan cinematic reference’lar.
- Kadrlar oddiy card emas: full-screen media, katta markaziy obyekt va kuchli atmosferaga ega.
- Harakatlar slow zoom, forward camera move, depth/parallax va floating object hissini beradi.
- UI juda kam; asosiy urg‘u media, rang, yorug‘lik va typographic overlay’da.

## 2. Kliplar bo‘yicha kuzatuv

- **Klip 1 — yacht / sunset:** issiq peach, oltin quyosh, sokin premium kayfiyat, sekin forward motion.
- **Klip 2 — night skyline:** navy/black fon, amber yo‘l chiroqlari, kuchli perspective va futuristik 3D depth.
- **Klip 3 — hanging chair:** markaziy inson, warm lifestyle, yumshoq ritm, tabiiy va ishonchli his.
- **Klip 4 — floating burger:** qora studio fon, isolated product, 3D floating/rotation va kuchli kontrast.
- **Klip 5 — flower field:** dreamy nature, saturated warm ranglar, forward movement va ko‘p qatlamli foreground/background.

## 3. GUZAL saytiga moslashtirilgan yo‘nalish

- Hero: toza portrait, kam dekor, warm ivory va blush ranglar.
- Jarayon section: to‘q cocoa/navy cinematic stage, katta video, 3D depth frame va soft light sweep.
- Motion: section reveal, yengil parallax, slow media movement, hover’da depth.
- 3D: og‘ir WebGL o‘rniga performant CSS perspective, radial light, layered frame va grain ishlatiladi.
- Maqsad: reference’larning premium cinematic ruhini saqlab, saytni kosmetologiya brendiga mos va tez ishlaydigan qilish.
